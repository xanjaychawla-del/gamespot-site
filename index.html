Game Spot

Seven free puzzle, word and classic games in one Android app. Play a quick round whenever you have a short break.

Coming soon to Google Play.

The games

  • Word GuessFind the hidden 5-letter word in six tries.
  • SudokuFresh puzzles on Easy, Medium or Hard.
  • 2048Slide and merge tiles to reach 2048.
  • Word WheelSpell words from a letter wheel to fill a mini crossword.
  • Tic-Tac-ToePlay a friend or the computer.
  • Memory MatchFlip cards to find matching pairs.
  • Brick PuzzleStack and clear falling bricks before they reach the top.

Why Game Spot

  • All games free to play
  • Quick rounds for short breaks
  • Runs on older phones (Android 7.0+) and tablets
  • Add friends and see your play counts
  • New games added over time
  • Optional “No Ads” subscription (Rs 10/month in India)

Game Spot is ad-supported: a short ad may appear after a round. Internet connection and an account are required. For ages 13+.
