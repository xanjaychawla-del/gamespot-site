PlayJar — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026 (draft for app 1.0.6 — not yet published)

This Privacy Policy explains how PlayJar (the "App", Android package com.xanjay.gamespot) handles your information. PlayJar was previously called "Game Spot". PlayJar is developed and operated by Sanjay Chawla, an individual developer based in India ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, contact us at xanjay.chawla@gmail.com.

PlayJar is a free collection of casual games (a 5-letter word-guessing game, 2048, Tic-Tac-Toe, Sudoku, Word Wheel, Brick Puzzle and Memory Match). It is supported by ads, and offers an optional paid subscription that removes ads.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you give us

  • Account information. You need an account to use PlayJar. When you create one we collect:
    • your email address;
    • if you sign up with email: a password, which we store only as a one-way salted hash (bcrypt). We never store your password in readable form;
    • if you sign in with Google or Facebook (or Sign in with Apple on iOS): the email address that provider shares with us and the provider's account identifier for you. We do not receive your password for those services, and we do not store your name or profile photo.
  • Friends. If you use the Friends feature, we store the friend requests you send and receive and your list of accepted friends. To send a request you search for other players by their email address.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback from Options > Send feedback, we store the type you pick (bug, idea or other), your message, an optional star rating, and, only if you choose to give it, your email address so we can reply. We also store the app version, platform (Android/iOS/web) and the random installation ID, and your account ID if you are signed in. You can send feedback without signing in.
  • Game suggestions. If you use Suggest a game, we store the game name, description and the optional example or link you type, with the app version, platform, installation ID and (if signed in) your account ID. You can suggest a game without signing in.
  • Your game library. The games you add to "My Games" are saved on your device. If you are signed in, the list of added games is also saved to your account so it follows you to other devices.

1.2 Information collected automatically when you use the App

  • Gameplay records. Each time you finish a round (win, lose or draw) the App records which game you played, the date and time, and your local calendar date. We use this to show your play counts and "recently played" order, and to decide when an ad is due.
  • Word Wheel word reports. When you try a valid-looking word in Word Wheel that is not in that level's word list, the App sends us the word, the level number and the level's letters so we can improve the word lists.
  • Daily rewards and streak freezes. If you are signed in, we store your daily-reward coin balance, the day of your 7-day reward calendar, the date you last claimed, how many streak freezes you hold and have used, and the date you last earned a freeze by watching an ad, so they follow you to other devices. If you are not signed in, these stay on your device only.
  • Daily Challenge results and streaks. When you finish a Daily Word or Daily Sudoku we store the date, whether you solved it, your guesses or mistakes, your time, a coloured summary grid (it does not contain the answer) and your current and best streak. Daily leaderboards show other players only a shortened form of your email address's first part (for example "san***"), never the full address.
  • App usage events (our own analytics). The App sends us simple usage events: app opened and closed (session start and end, with a random session ID and how long the session lasted), game started and finished or quit (game, result, score, duration), games added to or removed from My Games, ads and reward videos shown or rewarded (and where in the App), daily challenge completed, and subscription purchase started or completed. Each event carries the app version, platform and a random installation ID created by the App (not your advertising ID or any other device identifier). Events are stored on your device and sent when you are online. If you are signed in, events are linked to your account. We use them only to understand which features are used and how many players return, and to improve the App. We do not sell them or share them with advertisers.
  • Gameplay analytics (Firebase / Google Analytics). From app version 1.0.8 the App also collects gameplay analytics events (screens viewed, game starts and finishes, ad and purchase events) through Google's Firebase / Google Analytics for Firebase SDK to help us improve the games. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy; we do not use it to identify you or for advertising.
  • Device information (for analytics). With usage events the App sends your device's brand and model (for example "Samsung SM-A515F"), Android version, whether it is a phone or a tablet, and your language/region setting (for example "en-IN"). This does not include your advertising ID, IMEI, serial number or any other device identifier.
  • Approximate location (country and city). When our server receives usage events, it derives your approximate country and city from the network request (using the geolocation headers our hosting provider adds) and stores only that country and city with the event. We do not store your IP address with analytics events, and we never collect precise (GPS) location.
  • Time zone. Your device's time zone setting (for example "Asia/Kolkata") is sent when you sign up or sign in, so that daily statistics follow your local day. It is also sent with usage events.
  • Subscription records. If you subscribe to "PlayJar No Ads", we receive from Google Play the subscription's product, its purchase token, its status (active, grace period, expired, cancelled) and its expiry date, so we can verify the purchase and remove ads. We also receive and store Google Play's subscription status notifications (which contain the purchase token).
  • Sign-in tokens. To keep you signed in, the App stores sign-in tokens in your device's secure storage (Android Keystore). Our server stores only a hashed copy of your refresh token.
  • Advertising data (collected by Google AdMob). See section 3.
  • Game catalog and downloads. The Browse screen downloads the list of available games from our server. If you add a downloadable game, the App downloads that game's files from our storage provider (Supabase) and keeps them on your device until you remove the game; like any download, this request includes your IP address. Downloaded games run offline inside the App and can report only a score or result back to the App.
  • Server logs. Like most online services, our servers and hosting provider may record technical information such as your IP address and request times, and error details, to operate and secure the service.

1.3 Information stored only on your device

Some data never leaves your device: your Word Wheel level and coin balance, your app settings (sound, vibration, theme, colour-blind mode), best scores and saved games, the games in your library (unless you are signed in, see 1.1), downloaded game files, feedback waiting to be sent while offline, and an offline copy of your Daily Challenge results and streak. The games themselves run on your device.

1.4 What we do not collect

We do not request access to your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location. We do not collect your real name, phone number, or payment card details (payments are handled entirely by Google Play).

2. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • create and secure your account, sign you in and keep you signed in;
  • run the games and show your play counts and recently played games;
  • run the Friends feature;
  • show pop-up (interstitial) and banner ads to players who have not subscribed, and decide how often they appear;
  • offer optional reward videos, which any player (subscribers included) can choose to watch for extra hints and undos;
  • verify your subscription and remove pop-up and banner ads for subscribers;
  • understand, in aggregate, which games and features are used, on which kinds of devices and in which countries and cities, so we can improve the App;
  • review game suggestions and improve word lists;
  • prevent fraud and abuse, fix bugs, and keep the service working;
  • respond to your requests and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob) and the advertising ID

If you do not subscribe, PlayJar shows a full-screen (interstitial) ad from Google AdMob after finished rounds (how often is a setting we control on our server, for example after every round or every few rounds).

  • Google's ad SDK in the App automatically collects and shares with Google your device's advertising ID and app set ID, your IP address (which can be used to estimate your general location), interactions with ads and the App (such as app launches and ad taps/views) and diagnostic information (such as app performance). Google uses this to serve and measure ads, for analytics, and to prevent fraud. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Consent. Before ads load, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and certain US states). You can choose personalised or non-personalised ads there. If consent is required and not given, the App may show only limited or no ads.
  • Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).
  • Subscribers. If you have an active "PlayJar No Ads" subscription, the App shows no pop-up (interstitial) or banner ads. Subscribers may optionally choose to watch reward videos for extra hints and undos; these are only shown when you tap to watch one. When a subscriber watches a reward video, Google's ad SDK collects the data described above for that video.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your data for up to 2 years after your last activity, and then delete it automatically. "Activity" means signing in or using the App while signed in (for example playing, finishing a Daily Challenge, claiming a daily reward, or syncing your library). A daily automated job on our server enforces this:

  • Accounts. If a signed-in account has had no activity for 2 years, the account and all data linked to it are deleted, exactly as if you had deleted it yourself (section 6). Accounts with an active or in-grace subscription are not removed while the subscription is active.
  • App usage events are deleted 2 years after we receive them. When you delete your account they are deleted or unlinked from it.
  • Account, friends, game library, gameplay records, Daily Challenge results and streaks, daily rewards and streak freezes, Word Wheel word reports and subscription records are kept while your account is active, deleted 2 years after your last activity, and deleted immediately when you delete your account (section 6).
  • Feedback and game suggestions are deleted 2 years after you send them. Feedback linked to your account is deleted when you delete your account; game suggestions are unlinked from your account. To have feedback you sent without signing in deleted sooner, email us the approximate date and content.
  • Google Play subscription notifications we receive from Google are not linked to your account by name or email, and are deleted after 2 years.
  • Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days of inactivity on a device; signing out revokes the session.
  • Server logs are kept for a limited period by our hosting provider for security and troubleshooting.
  • Data stored only on your device (when you are not signed in) stays on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it.
  • If we build a game from your suggestion, we may credit you by a display name in that game; that credit may remain after you delete your account. Tell us if you'd like it removed.
  • Data collected by Google AdMob is retained under Google's policies.

6. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time:

  1. In the App: open Profile (the person icon on the Game Library screen) and tap Delete account, then confirm. This immediately and permanently deletes your account and its associated data from our server: your email, password hash, sign-in links, friends and friend requests, game library, feedback, gameplay records, Daily Challenge results and streaks, daily rewards and streak freezes, Word Wheel word reports and subscription records, and unlinks your app usage events and game suggestions from your account.
  2. By email: if you can't access the App, email xanjay.chawla@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my PlayJar account". We may ask you to confirm the request, and we will delete your account and data within 30 days.

Important: deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions to stop being charged. Uninstalling the App removes the data stored only on your device.

You can also ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it by emailing us. Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority.

7. Children

PlayJar is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the higher minimum age that applies in your country). If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us at xanjay.chawla@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. Security

We protect your information with reasonable measures, including: encrypted HTTPS connections between the App and our server; passwords stored only as salted bcrypt hashes; refresh tokens stored only as hashes; short-lived access tokens; and sign-in tokens kept in your device's secure storage. Purchases are verified directly with Google. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International transfers

We are based in India, and our servers and service providers (including Google) may process data in India or other countries. Where required, we take steps to protect your information when it is transferred.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add features. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the App or on the store listing.
